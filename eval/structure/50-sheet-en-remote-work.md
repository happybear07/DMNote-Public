# Remote Work Survival Guide

## Set up your space
- A desk you only use for work
- A chair that supports your back
- Good light in front of you, not behind

## Daily routine
Start at the same time every day → block two hours for deep work → take a real lunch break → write tomorrow's top three tasks

## Communication
- Post a short status in the team channel every morning
- Turn the camera on for one-on-ones

## Avoid burnout
> Warning: if you answer messages after 9 pm every night, you are not working remotely, you are always working.

## Useful tools
Slack, Notion, Toggl, Krisp
